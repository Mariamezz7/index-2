Pain First, Product Second
Every script leads with the prospect's specific pain — never open with SAP features or capabilities.
🇸🇦
Localise Immediately
SFDA, MEWA, Vision 2030, and KSA references (NADEC, SALIC, Tanmiah) in the first 30 seconds.
📊
Anchor with Numbers
Use benchmarks: 25%, 30%, 40% — never make a promise without a proof point benchmark.
📞
One Clear CTA
End with a single ask: a 30-minute demo or discovery call. No vague "let's stay in touch."
⏱
Under 90 Seconds
Opening calls only — you're earning the meeting, not closing the deal. Keep it sharp and tight.
🏆
Local References First
NADEC, SALIC, Tanmiah carry far more weight in KSA than Dole, Tyson, or global-only names.

1

Account Identification

Pull from ONE360 + ZoomInfo profiling of white space accounts. Output: enriched CSVs segmented by industry.

2

Account Segmentation

Split by industry (Agri / Wholesale / Retail) and sub-industry. Assign account batches to relevant partner.

3

Contact Enrichment

Use ZoomInfo to find CEO/CFO/COO direct contacts. Validate and connect via LinkedIn (Dripify).

4

Sequence Launch

Load contacts into Outreach (email cadence) and Dripify (LinkedIn drip). Use industry-specific sequences per persona.

5

Partner Briefing / Enablement

1-hr session: pitch training, objection handling, calling tips. Share one-pager PDFs & scripts per industry.

6

In-person sprint at SAP KSA Office or remote. SDE provides live floor coaching. All calls logged in CRM in real time.

Lead Creation

8

Opportunity Progression & AE Handover

SDE supports warm handover to AE/TEM. Opportunity created in CRM with full context from discovery call.

Bi-Weekly Reporting

☑ 8-Point Qualification Checklist — Gate to Opportunity

All 8 criteria must be addressed
1
Criterion 01 — Pain Confirmed
Business Pain Identified & Articulated
The prospect has expressed a clear, specific business problem that GROW with SAP can solve. Not a generic interest — a named pain (e.g. "SFDA audit preparation takes 3 weeks", "cold chain breaks cost us 20% losses").
Mandatory
2
Criterion 02 — Decision Maker
C-Level / Economic Buyer Engaged
We have spoken directly with the CEO, CFO, COO, or the person who controls the budget decision. Contact with an IT manager or coordinator only does not qualify — we need the economic buyer in the conversation.
Mandatory
3
Criterion 03 — Budget Awareness
Budget Exists or Can Be Allocated
The prospect has indicated budget availability, an active digital transformation project, or a willingness to explore investment. No budget signal at all = nurture, not qualify. Look for: transformation initiatives, Vision 2030 funding, CAPEX cycle, board-approved projects.
Important
4
Criterion 04 — Timeline
Decision / Go-Live Timeline Identified
The prospect has given an indication of when they would want to go live or make a decision — even a rough range ("we're looking at H1 next year", "board decision in Q4"). No timeline = track as interest, not opportunity.
Important
5
Criterion 05 — Current State
Current System / Gap Understood
We know what system (or lack thereof) the prospect is running today: spreadsheets, legacy ERP (ECC, Oracle, Dynamics), or no system. This confirms the displacement or greenfield opportunity and shapes the pitch for the discovery meeting.
Required
6
Criterion 06 — Next Step Agreed
Concrete Next Action Confirmed in Diary
A specific next step has been agreed and placed in the calendar: discovery call, demo, workshop, or meeting with the SAP team. A vague "let's stay in touch" or "send me something" does not qualify. The meeting invite must be sent and accepted.
Mandatory
7
Criterion 07 — Company Fit
Company Size & Industry Fit GROW with SAP
The prospect fits the GROW with SAP profile: midsize company (typically SAR 50M–2B revenue), not a global enterprise requiring RISE. Industry must be in scope: Agribusiness, Mill & Manufacturing, Food Manufacturing, or Retail. Confirm headcount and revenue range.
Fit Check
8
Criterion 08 — No Blocking Factors
No Known Blockers to Progressing
There are no known blockers: no active competing vendor selected, no recent failed SAP project, no internal freeze on IT spend, no pending M&A that would delay decisions. If a blocker exists, document it and set a follow-up date — do not log as an opportunity.
Risk Check

✅ Solid Booked Account — Definition
A Solid Booked Account means all 8 qualification criteria are addressed, the economic buyer is engaged, a confirmed meeting is in the AE/TEM diary, and the opportunity is created in ONE360 with full context. This is the moment the account moves from SDE/Partner ownership to AE ownership.
